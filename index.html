01The challenge

Differentiating Jio-bp in a commoditised category

Fuel is widely regarded as a commodity, with customers choosing stations largely on price and convenience. Jio-bp set out to differentiate through the complete customer experience: advanced fuels, EV charging, convenience retail and rewarding loyalty programmes.

Presence

Strengthen Jio-bp's brand presence and visibility across India.

Fuel

Differentiate its fuels through Active Technology and performance-led stories.

EV

Build awareness of EV charging and emerging mobility solutions.

Loyalty

Drive engagement for Jio-bp Forever and the Mobility+ Credit Card.

Convenience

Promote convenience-led experiences through Wild Bean Cafe and mobility stations.

Communication

Keep every message relatable and consumer-first, on every platform.

07Impact

Impact and key outcomes

By translating product features into relatable customer moments, the communication made Jio-bp's offerings clearer, more relevant and easier to act on.

  • Stronger product communication across fuels, EV charging, café, loyalty and new services.
  • Consumer-first storytelling around fuel performance, EV charging and mobility benefits.
  • A simpler story for earning and redeeming Smiles across Jio-bp Forever and Mobility+.
  • One consistent communication system from digital to retail to on-ground.
  • New formats that travel well: WhatsApp series, GIFs, reels and sticker packs.

Campaign reach, engagement and Smiles redemption metrics will be added as platform data becomes available.
